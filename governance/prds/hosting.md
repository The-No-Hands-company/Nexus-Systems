# Nexus Hosting PRD

**Owner:** Founder
**Maturity baseline:** L3
**Release target:** L4

## Product purpose

Nexus Hosting lets people deploy static sites with managed public URLs and
custom domains while integrating with the Nexus control plane.

## Verified current scope

- Static-site hosting and deployment lifecycle.
- Public URL and custom-domain capabilities.
- Integration with Cloud routing and the production deployment stack.

## P1 requirements

- Evidence-backed acceptance for upload, deploy, publish, and rollback.
- Clear failure and recovery states for deployment and public delivery.

## P2 requirements

- Product-level deployment history and operator-facing release diagnostics.

## Release acceptance

- Hosting's production checklist and app quality gate pass.
- Nit and staging verify a deploy, public access, rollback, and recovery.
- Founder approves the release and records it in the changelog.
